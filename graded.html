Methodology — why TAG values, not PSA

TAG slabs trade below same-grade PSA. Rather than assume a discount, it was measured: Scrydex charts individual TAG sales per grade. Across the seven cases where one of these exact cards has both a TAG sale and a PSA price at the identical grade, TAG cleared a median 64% of PSA:
